Subject: Deviation - Batch KT-24-0187 Amlodipine Besylate - Assay/Impurity OOS after crystallisation

Product: Amlodipine Besylate API
Batch No.: KT-24-0187
Stage: Crystallisation (Reactor R-304) followed by centrifuge CF-12 and tray drying

During in-process control after crystallisation on 11 Oct 2026 the HPLC result for Impurity B was 0.31% against a specification of NMT 0.15%. The batch was put on hold. Assay was 98.2% (spec 99.0-101.0%).

Observations:
- The cooling ramp on R-304 was recorded as 20 C to 5 C in 45 minutes; the approved BMR specifies 20 C to 5 C over 3 hours.
- The jacket temperature controller TIC-304 was replaced by maintenance on 9 Oct 2026. The post-maintenance requalification record has not been attached to the batch file.
- Operator on shift (B. Singh) joined the department 3 weeks ago; training on the revised cooling profile (SOP-CR-021 rev 04) is not yet documented.
- Seed crystals lot SC-0912 were used; the CoA shows particle size within limits.
- Ambient humidity in the block was normal. No alarms were recorded on the SCADA during the step.

Immediate actions: batch KT-24-0187 placed on quality hold, reactor R-304 isolated, retain samples drawn and re-tested in duplicate (confirming the OOS). Neighbouring batch KT-24-0188 held pending review.

Potential impact: possible rejection or reprocessing of 412 kg of API. No product has been dispatched. Risk to patient is considered low since the batch is on hold.

Reported by Priya Nair on 12 Oct 2026. Classified as Major.
